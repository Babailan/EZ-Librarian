# 📘 EZ Librarian

**EZ Librarian** is a Minecraft mod that makes enchanting easier and less frustrating. When a player tries to reroll a novice librarian villager, the mod checks for a wall sign placed in front of the lectern. Based on the text written on the sign, the villager will attempt to offer that enchantment — letting players target specific books like Mending, Fortune, or Sharpness without endless rerolling.

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 Lets players choose enchantments by simply placing a **wall sign** in front of the **lectern**.
- 👨‍🏫 Works **only** on **novice librarian villagers** (ensuring fair balance).
- 📖 The **first line** of the sign determines the desired enchantment.
- 🔁 When the player breaks and replaces the lectern, the mod reads the sign and sets the librarian's trade accordingly (if valid).
- 💡 Saves hours of trial and error when hunting for enchantments like `Mending`, `Fortune`, or `Efficiency`.




> ✅ Only works if the librarian is still **novice** (hasn’t been traded with).
